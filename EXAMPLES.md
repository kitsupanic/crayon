# Examples: Anyquarium

Real rewrites from Anyquarium, an underwater game where you play a shark. "Boost" is the player's sprint move, so it stays as a keep-word.

## Upgrade cards

Picked on level-up. The cards show up three at a time, so every line reads differently.

| Card | Original | Grade 5 | Grade 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gullet | Swallow quicker — a fuller mouth, sooner | Swallow fish faster | Eat fish faster |
| Deep Lungs | Longer boost — the budget is the burst | Your boost lasts longer | Your boost lasts longer |
| Second Wind | Shorter waits between bursts | Your boost fills back up faster | Your boost comes back faster |
| Ambusher | Paid for creeping — the bar fills faster while stalking | Your boost fills up even faster while you sneak | Your boost comes back faster when you sneak |
| Quick Rearm | Boost comes back off a shallower bar | Boost again sooner after you run out | Boost again sooner when you run out |
| Wide Jaw | A wider bite — the same pass catches more | Bigger bite — catch more fish at once | Bigger mouth. Catch more fish. |
| Thick Skin | A deeper pool — one more band on the ring | More health | More health |
| Rich Blood | Every fish heals more | Each fish heals you more | Fish heal you more |
| Butcher | Every diver heals more | Each diver heals you more | Divers heal you more |
| Iron Grip | Tear quicker — less time held still under the guns | Tear divers apart faster | Rip divers apart faster |

## Shop upgrades

Permanent upgrades bought between runs. They're on a different screen from the cards, so they may match a card's line.

| Upgrade | Original | Grade 5 | Grade 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bone Plating | Start tougher — more pool to spend on spears | Start every run with more health | Start with more health |
| Born Lungs | Start with more burst in the budget | Start every run with a longer boost | Start with a longer boost |
| Quick Blood | Get the burst back sooner | Your boost fills back up faster | Your boost comes back faster |
| Rich Feeding | Every fish is worth more health | Each fish heals you more | Fish heal you more |
| Long Reach | Grab a diver from further out | Grab divers from farther away | Grab divers from farther away |

## What changed, and why

- **Code words left.** "Budget", "pool", "band on the ring" and "shallower bar" came from the design notes. Players never see them.
- **Dashes split or cut.** "Swallow quicker — a fuller mouth, sooner" said one thing twice. The short half was enough.
- **Metaphors became facts.** "Under the guns" and "paid for creeping" became what actually happens.
- **Grade 3 went one step smaller.** "Swallow" became "eat", "fills back up" became "comes back", and "Each fish heals you" became "Fish heal you".
